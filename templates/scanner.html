INTERNET SAFETY

How to stay safe on the internet

A few practical habits can protect your accounts, devices, and personal information.

01

Protect your accounts

Use a different, strong password for each account and turn on multi-factor authentication. Never share sign-in codes.

02

Check before you click

Use a known bookmark or type the website address yourself when a message asks you to sign in. HTTPS encrypts a connection but does not prove a site is trustworthy.

03

Keep devices updated

Install updates for your phone, computer, browser, and apps. Updates often fix security weaknesses.

04

Share less personal information

Be cautious with unexpected requests for payment details, passwords, identity documents, or personal information.

05

Be careful with downloads

Only open attachments and install software you were expecting, from a source you can verify.

06

Use public Wi-Fi thoughtfully

Avoid sensitive transactions on networks you do not trust, and use your mobile connection when unsure.

INTERNET SAFETY

How to spot a phishing email

Pause and verify before replying, clicking a link, or opening an attachment.

01

Notice pressure and urgency

Threats, deadlines, prizes, and “act now” demands are common ways to make you skip careful checks.

02

Check who sent it

Read the full sender address. A familiar display name can hide an unrelated or misspelled email address.

03

Verify links without opening them

On a computer, hover to inspect the destination; on a phone, press and hold carefully. If the address looks unfamiliar, do not open it.

04

Never send passwords or codes

Do not reply with passwords, payment information, or one-time verification codes. Go to the official website or app instead.

05

Treat unexpected attachments carefully

Do not open an attachment you were not expecting, even if the message appears to come from someone you know.

06

Verify through another channel

Contact the person or organization using a phone number or website you already trust, not the details in the suspicious message.
