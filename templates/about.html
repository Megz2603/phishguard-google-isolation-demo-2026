GET TO KNOW PHISHGUARD

Making digital security smarter.

PhishGuard is an email security application designed to help users identify suspicious emails, understand potential phishing threats and make safer decisions when interacting with their inbox.

Why PhishGuard?

Phishing remains a common cybersecurity threat. Attackers use deceptive emails to trick individuals into revealing sensitive information, clicking malicious links or downloading harmful attachments. PhishGuard aims to make identifying suspicious email activity easier by analysing email characteristics and highlighting potential warning signs.

Our Purpose

Our purpose is to provide users with a practical tool that supports safer email practices. By presenting risk assessments and organising suspicious messages into categories, PhishGuard helps users make more informed decisions about the emails they receive.

What PhishGuard Offers

Email Analysis

Examines email characteristics to identify indicators commonly associated with phishing.

Risk Scoring

Assigns risk scores to help users understand the potential level of concern associated with an email.

Email Organisation

Separates emails into categories such as inbox, review and quarantine to support easier email management.
